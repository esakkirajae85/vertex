Franchise Risks & Reality

We believe in complete transparency. Here's an honest look at franchise risks — and how Vertex helps you minimize them.

01

Limited Freedom

Must follow company rules, pricing, and branding. Not for those who want total independence.

02

Investment & Fees

Franchise fee, setup cost, and ongoing charges. Income recovery takes time if growth is slow.

03

No Guaranteed Income

Earnings depend on location, effort, and marketing. Success varies by partner.

04

Brand Dependency

Your business is tied to the brand's reputation. A strong parent brand like Vertex reduces this risk.

05

Location Risk

Wrong location means fewer clients. High-rent areas can pressure profits.

06

Time to Grow

First months may be slow. Patience and long-term vision are essential for success.
